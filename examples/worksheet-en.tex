\section*{1. Equal parts (grades 5--6)}
Calculate $\frac34+\frac16$ using a common denominator. Explain why equal-sized parts are necessary.
\section*{2. Function and check (secondary school)}
For $f(x)=x^2-4x+3$, find the real roots and vertex. Check the roots in the original expression; describe the graph without relying on color.
\section*{3. Complex roots (introductory university)}
Find all $z\in\mathbb C$ such that $z^3=8i$. State the modulus and the three arguments. Verify each root.
\section*{4. Matrix action (introductory university)}
Let $A=\begin{pmatrix}2&1\\1&2\end{pmatrix}$. Compute $Av$ for $v=(1,1)^\mathsf T$ and $v=(1,-1)^\mathsf T$. Interpret the scaling.
